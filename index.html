Questions before you begin.

A few useful things to understand before comparing walk-in tub products and installation options.

A walk-in tub is designed with an access door and a lower entry point so the user does not need to step over a conventional high bathtub wall.

In many bathrooms, replacing an existing tub may be possible. The exact approach depends on available space, plumbing, access and the selected walk-in tub configuration.

Useful points to compare include entry height, seat dimensions, door direction, grab bars, controls, drainage, shower accessories and optional hydrotherapy features.
