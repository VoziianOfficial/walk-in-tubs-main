Introduction

This Cookies Policy describes how cookies and similar technologies may be used when you browse or interact with this website.

Cookies can support website functionality, remember certain settings and provide information about how visitors use a website.

02

What Cookies Are

Cookies are small data files that websites may store on a browser or device when a visitor accesses a website.

They can contain information used to recognise a browser, remember preferences or help a website operate more efficiently.

Similar technologies may perform comparable functions without necessarily using a traditional browser cookie.

03

How Cookies May Be Used

Cookies and similar technologies may be used for operational, security, preference and website performance purposes.

They may help to:

  • Support normal website functionality
  • Remember selected website preferences
  • Maintain appropriate website security
  • Understand how visitors navigate the website
  • Identify technical issues and improve website performance
04

Types of Cookies

Different cookies may serve different purposes and may remain on a device for different periods of time.

Common categories include:

  • Essential or strictly necessary cookies
  • Preference or functionality cookies
  • Performance and analytics cookies
  • Security-related cookies

Not every category described here is necessarily active at all times. The technologies used on the website may change as website functionality changes.

05

Essential Cookies

Some cookies may be required for basic website functions, security or features requested by the visitor.

Blocking essential technologies through browser settings may affect the way certain parts of a website operate.

06

Analytics and Performance

Analytics technologies may be used to understand how visitors interact with the website.

This type of information may include general usage patterns such as visited pages, browser or device information and interactions with website features.

Such information may be used to identify areas of the website that can be improved.

07

Third-Party Technologies

Some website functions may rely on third-party services that can use their own cookies or similar technologies.

These services may include website hosting, analytics, embedded content, security or other technical functionality.

Third-party services operate according to their own policies and practices.

08

Managing Cookies

Most modern browsers provide settings that allow users to view, delete or restrict cookies.

Depending on your browser, you may be able to:

  • Delete previously stored cookies
  • Block cookies from particular websites
  • Restrict third-party cookies
  • Configure notifications before cookies are stored

Restricting cookies may change how some website features behave.

09

Changes to This Policy

This Cookies Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in website functionality, technologies or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, an updated version may be published on this page.
